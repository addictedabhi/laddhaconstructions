About

The firm and the people behind it

Civil construction, delivered to specification.

Laddha Constructions is a Kota-based civil construction firm working across buildings, roads, highways and industrial roads. Since 2008 the firm has served private developers, contractors and government bodies — the PWD, the Urban Improvement Trust, Nagar Nigam, the Marketing Board and RIICO among them.

Work spans the full road train — excavation, granular sub-base, wet mix macadam, bituminous macadam and concrete surfacing — alongside township drainage, masonry and hume pipe works, industrial site infrastructure with its electrical scope, and the conservation of Rajasthan’s historic stepwells.

Material is checked at the firm’s own inspection unit before it reaches the site, and every service is committed to the client’s specification and schedule.

Leadership

Mr. Ashok Laddha

The firm is managed and guided by Mr. Ashok Laddha, whose thirty-six years in civil engineering services anchor its planning, execution and client relationships. Day-to-day site management runs through a permanent team of engineers, supervisors and operators who move between the firm’s active works.
