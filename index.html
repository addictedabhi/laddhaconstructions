The firm

Eighteen years of building in the Hadoti region.

Laddha Constructions provides civil construction services across buildings, roads, highways and industrial roads, for private developers and government bodies alike. From a base in Kota, the firm has delivered township road networks, flyover surfacing, industrial townships and heritage restoration work across Rajasthan.

What we build

Roads & highways

Excavation, GSB, WMM, bituminous macadam and concrete surfacing — complete road trains from formation to final coat.

Township infrastructure

Internal road networks, drainage, hume pipe and masonry works for residential and coaching-hub townships.

Industrial & electrical

Industrial townships with water supply, overhead power lines, 32 kV GSS and 33 kV feeder installation.

Heritage restoration

Conservation of historic bawaris — stepwells — in stone, for municipal and development authorities.
